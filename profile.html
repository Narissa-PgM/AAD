03 · Mission and values

One Harvard, applied to stewardship

Proposed stewardship leadership mission: coordinate people, finance, program evidence, systems and communications so that decentralized reporting meets one understandable standard—without claiming formal authority over schools or offices.

Operating method

One Harvard

Build consensus with gift officers, Finance, academic units, Advancement Services and leadership. Shared visibility, local accountability.

Execution discipline

SMARTER

Specific standards, measurable baselines, attainable rollout, relevant impact, time-bound cadence, evaluation and review.

Publication control

Six-Check Release Gate

Intent · Reconciliation · Consent · Traceability · Attribution · Authorization. Unresolved evidence means HOLD.

Editorial structure

VERITAS

Vision · Experience · Results · Investment · Truth · Advancement · Stewardship. A proposed donor-story mnemonic, not an official interpretation of Harvard’s motto.

AA&D values—respect, inclusion, trust, collaboration, continuous improvement and open communication—inform behavior and review culture; they are distinct from the editorial and release frameworks.

05 · Practical execution

How the first-year plan earns confidence

Illustrative delivery gates—subject to actual baselines, capacity, available systems and approval authority.

Days 1–30 · Listen and baseline

Inventory reporting commitments, interview gift officers and school contacts, record actual data sources, review donor intent and calendar peaks, and assess current report quality.

Gate: agreed problem and evidence baseline

Days 31–60 · Standardize and pilot

Design four reporting-family templates and a claim-to-source register. Pilot narrative and financial QA with Finance and relationship owners; publish a visible exception queue.

Gate: acceptance tests and named reviewers

Days 61–90 · Decide and scale carefully

Evaluate pilot results, workload and donor usefulness. Approve a phased delivery calendar only where source quality, consent and security requirements are satisfied.

Gate: go / rework / stop decision

Performance goals belong to the future authorized implementation baseline, not to the fictional donor cases in the prototype.

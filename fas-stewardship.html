S

Specific · purpose, audience, owner, scope

The decision, the owner, and the number

Every reporting season, FAS stewardship staff spend an estimated 40 hours per batch of 25 endowed-fund reports gathering fund financials, beneficiary names and stories from separate systems, reconciling them to the ledger, and drafting, because there is no shared check before drafting begins. The decision this pilot supports: which report families are ready for a drafting assist and the digital platform, and where staff time should go first.

M

Measurable · baseline, KPIs, evidence

Who benefits, what changes, by how much, and the test

We believe a human-in-the-loop preparation workflow, run in shadow mode on endowed scholarship reports and then scaled to four families, will deliver reports on time with no release errors and cut preparation hours by 30% or more within one reporting season. AI never sends a report and never supplies a figure of its own.

Who benefits

Stewardship writers and coordinators, gift officers, and the donors who read the reports.

What changes

Facts arrive checked and source-tagged before anyone writes a sentence.

By how much

Preparation time down 30% or more; on-time delivery at 95% or better.

The test

Quality, adoption and impact targets all pass on two batches in a row.

The reviewer checks, every time

  • Every figure has a source tag and ties to the ledger.
  • Purpose and naming match the gift agreement.
  • No name or story without a consent flag; anonymity requests honored.
  • The personal paragraph sounds like Harvard, not a template.
  • Each edit gets a reason code.

Nothing sends without a named approver. AI never supplies a figure.

What the AI may and may not do

✓On its own

  • Pull read-only data from approved sources
  • Match amounts to the ledger and flag variances
  • Draft from tagged facts and approved templates
  • Suggest a story from the consented library

!Only with a person

  • Any figure or name that reaches the final
  • Every edit accepted into the report
  • Template and prompt changes
  • Release, with a named approver

×Never

  • Send or schedule a report
  • Change a source record
  • Invent or estimate a figure
  • Use data outside the approved environment

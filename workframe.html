Yearly cycle

Gate at every stage

SMARTER

Specific · Measurable · Attainable · Relevant · Time-bound · Evaluate · Review. Its control at Review & Certify is the Six-Check Release Gate.

Donor narrative structure

VERITAS

Vision · Experience · Results · Investment · Truth · Advancement · Stewardship. A proposed editorial mnemonic, not an official expansion of the University motto.

AA&D values · cultural standard: Respect, inclusion, trust, collaboration, continuous improvement and open communication.

Operating rule: One Harvard sets who works together; the Impact Circle sets when; SMARTER gates every stage, with the Six-Check Release Gate at Review & Certify; VERITAS structures what every report says.

How tone works on this page

The tone selector changes the voice, never the facts. Every variant of every message is generated from the same verified record: the certified statement, the aid ledger, the cohort file, the consented story, the current preferences.

Formal

Used for a leadership donor whose relationship is long and whose preference is a professional register. Complete sentences, no contractions, no exclamation marks, the honorific the CRM specifies.

Warm

Used for a donor whose record shows they have replied to past letters, attend events, or have explicitly asked to be called by first name. Plain prose, contractions, a single sentence of feeling.

Plain

Used when the donor is new, prefers email, or has asked for accessibility. Short sentences, no honorific, no adverbs, a reading level a fourteen-year-old can follow — and the same facts in the same order.

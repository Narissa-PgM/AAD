# Bridge Builder & Open-Door Hub — GitHub Pages module

Open `bridge-builder.html#today`. This is a **functional, standalone, browser-local prototype**, not a multi-user calendar. It accompanies the University Stewardship Operations Suite, Team Leadership, Program Office and Playbook.

## Features
- **Daily situation board:** date/unit/person/project/status filters; plan, heads-up, outcomes; done/blocked/postponed/canceled state; activity journal, people progress, exports.
- **Weekly group calendar:** daily work commitments and confirmed sample appointments, with unit/person/project and meeting/task filters.
- **Open-door appointments:** three proposed time options; 20/30/45-minute meetings; protected focus period and 15-minute buffer; local request/host confirmation; editable email draft and .ics export.
- **Whisper Box:** local idea cards labeled personal, project, internal or external and work/non-work; topic and audience filters. These labels **are not private sharing**, access permissions, or communication delivery.
- **Annual wheel:** clickable 12-month ring and seven-stage stewardship process with proposed timing, operational owners, and public academic calendar links.
- **Management insights:** data-derived unit completion and exception counts, executive brief export and demo reset.

## Data and limitations
All staff names, calendar commitments, status updates and conversations are **synthetic**. Data persists using `localStorage` within one browser only; opening this app on another device will not show the changes. No requests are sent; real meetings are not booked. Avoid entering actual donor information, identifiable personal information, confidential conversations, or institutional credentials in this public demo. An approved server/calendar/identity integration would be needed for secure real-time sharing.

## Calendar grounding
- Harvard FAS academic calendar: https://registrar.fas.harvard.edu/calendars (Fall 2026 term starts Sept 2, Spring 2027 term Jan 25, tentative)
- Harvard Commencement Office: https://commencement.harvard.edu/schedule (Commencement May 27, 2027)
- HBS Giving FAQ: https://www.alumni.hbs.edu/giving/Pages/default.aspx (June 30 fiscal-year deadline)
Seven stage windows are planning suggestions, not adopted Harvard scheduling policy. School and reunion calendars vary and must be verified prior to outreach.
